## Handling company information
- **Do not put confidential company originals in the repository.** Contracts, customer data and sales ledgers are examples.
  If needed, read them from the local path written in `CLAUDE.local.md` and keep **only a summary and the location** here.
- Record colleagues **by name only.** No phone numbers, addresses or other personal data.
- A private repository limits access; it is not protection against leaks. Check your organization's policy before putting work records in a personal repository.

<!--endif-->
## Safety
- **Confirm before destructive commands.** Deleting, overwriting and bulk moves count.
- Before running one, say **what changes and how** in one line and get approval.
